Hi, I’m Liraz. Thanks for finding your way here. Of all the embarrassing and uncomfortable things involved in building my own website, writing the “About Me” section is the worst of them all. Especially since the whole website is about me. About things I like, things I do, and, yes, things I do professionally, like mentoring PMs and teaching meditation.

In its slightly manipulative way, the website is already telling different stories about me. Showing different aspects of me. So what am I supposed to do here? What should I write?
 
**A few dry facts**

Liraz, 57, living in Berlin. I work as a product manager, write on Substack in Hebrew (my mother tongue), practice Buddhist meditation, and teach MBSR (Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction). I am an avid baker and an okay cook. I take long daily walks in the city. I lived in India for three years. I read tons of books in almost every genre. There’s a picture of me below.

And all of that is true, and also untrue.

You’re very welcome to discover more about me through this website. Each page shows a different aspect of me. Different possibilities. Different things I offer. Different ways I might be able to help. You can also get to know me through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lirazaxelrad/), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/liraz.axelrad) or [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/lirazax/). Or you can simply get in [touch](/en/contact) with me. That’s the easiest way.
