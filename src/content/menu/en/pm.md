I worked as a Product Manager for more than 20 years. When I started “working on the internet” as my mom called it, we experienced websites via [Netscape 2.0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netscape_Navigator_2) If you have never heard about it, ask Claude or the AI of your choice. 

When I took my first big job, to build and manage the first web site of Israel Open University, my best friend, who started working as an editor in Haaretz news paper days after graduating from Uni, said it was a role fit for a student. Obviously, she was wrong. The length and full details of my career are on [linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lirazaxelrad/) so I won't repeat them here. Trust me on that (long, good career), or go check yourself. 

Since then I’ve been a hands-on product manager at startups, hypergrowth and big companies, launching and managing successful web and mobile products. Recently I've focused on dealing with real world problems, bringing clarity and alignment to the messy spot where regulation meets human behavior. Examples: helping municipalities understand and optimize parking patterns in the city with EasyPark (now [Arrive](https://arrive.com/en)), and helping German users successfully submit their tax returns and secure accurate refunds with [Taxfix](https://taxfix.de/). Outside of my formal work I've mentored product managers (multiple cycles with [Product League](https://productleague.com/) and [Give & Tech](https://giveandtech.org.il/), primarily women).

**Now**

Right now I am not working. By choice. Throughout my career I took multiple periods of time off and dedicated myself to other things I love doing. I think it’s one of the benefits, or better yet, a privilege, of working in this field. One can take time off, if that is what one wants. I am, however, slowly and calmly looking for what's next for me. I hope my next role will be on a product and at a company that is doing something good in this world: solving real problems, making something hard and complex more accessible, connecting, helping, adding real value to our users, and of course, to the company. With a user-first frame of mind. 

**Until then**

I'm teaching myself AI, and enjoying it enormously. Here's my GitHub, where you'll find a bunch of small tools I've built: a Hebrew and English spell checker, a word interpreter, a summary editor. I'm also building bigger things, like an advisor for managing my own investment portfolio, but that one stays private. Everything you see on [GitHub](https://github.com/LirazAxelrad100), though, is completely open.

**Mentoring**

Over the years I've mentored product managers through [Product League](https://productleague.com/) and [Give & Tech](https://giveandtech.org.il/). The results: highly satisfied mentees, many of whom advanced their careers (moved up, changed jobs, grew professionally).

I'm offering myself as a mentor. If you need help with anything related to product management, a space to talk things through, to learn, to vent, to share, to talk shop and figure out how to do what, and also the things underneath the professional stuff: the uncomfortable conversation with your boss, the feedback you were hoping not to get, I'm here for that too. Reach out through the [form](/en/contact). First meeting, a half hour call, free, and then we'll see if it's a good fit to keep working together. I'd love to hear from you.
